# The Chess Sniper
This repo allows you to play with a human like bot against most people and when you play certain people enables max-strength stockfish. Good for streamsniping.<br>

## Dependencies:
The userscript must be installed with a userscript manager (tested with tampermonkey). Pip the requirements.txt (if you can't figure it out google it or smth) and requires stockfish to be installed (make sure to put the path you have stockfish at in config.json).

## Usuage:
Put who you want to target in your config and then when your ready to be a no life and cheat on chess, start the bridge.py, enable the userscript and go to chess.com. Then queue up and watch it work its magic.
